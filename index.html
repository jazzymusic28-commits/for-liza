Why I'm Blessed to Call You Mine

From Derrick's whole heart to Princess Liza

To My Dearest Princess Liza,

Having you as my girlfriend brings so much genuine peace to my life. You are my biggest supporter, my prayer partner, and without hesitation the sweetest part of my entire day.

Our Journey in Spirit

Seeing how deeply you love God, how humbly you carry yourself, and how sincerely you care for me makes Derrick thank Him every single morning when waking up. I love us, I love everything we are building together, and I love walking this journey with Princess Liza side by side in spirit every day.

An Answered Prayer

I want to take a moment and step past all the rush of daily life to put down in words how much I truly, deeply love you. When I look at you and think about the person you are, I see an answered prayer that God personally shaped for my life.

My feelings for you are not casual, surface-level emotions that change with time or mood—they are rooted in an intentional commitment to honor, cherish, and stand beside you. No matter how many miles, time zones, or oceans stretch between the United States and the Philippines, you have completely captivated my heart.

Emotional Safety & Venting

One of the things I treasure most about you is the emotional safety we share. You never have to put on a mask with me, Liza. You never have to act strong when you are tired, pretend everything is fine when you are sad, and you never have to worry that you are bothering me.

If you have a problem, let's talk about it. If you had a frustrating day, vent to me. If you are anxious about something, let's pray over it together. In this relationship, there is no judging, no impatience, and no walls. You have Derrick's full attention and an open ear whenever you need to let things out.

Your Strength & Rest

I admire you so much for the incredible strength, determination, and independence you carry. Watching how hard you push through long, tiring shifts, how disciplined you are, and how you carry your responsibilities with grace leaves me in awe of the woman you are.

You have a quiet power and resilience that is nothing short of inspiring. But I want to remind you of something very important: even though you are strong, and even though you know how to stand on your own two feet, you do not have to carry the weight of everything by yourself anymore.

You can lean on Derrick. You are allowed to take off the heavy armor when you talk to me. You don't always have to be the tough one, the dependable one, or the one who has all the answers. Let me be the person you can collapse into emotionally at the end of a hard day. Let me be your safe harbor, your peace, and your soft place to land.

Your Smile & Joy

Your smile is one of my favorite things in this world. The sound of your laugh, especially when you are being silly or playful, completely changes the energy of my day. It brightens up anything going on around me. Your voice brings peace to my spirit, and I always love connecting with you in real time.

My Promise to You 💍

Above everything else, Liza, please know in your bones: I am not here to break your heart, play games, or make you doubt yourself. I am here to protect your heart, support your growth, cherish your soul, and love you with real devotion. You have been hurt or cautious in life, but with Derrick, your heart is in gentle, loyal hands. I am here to walk through every chapter with you—the joyful sunny days, the exhausting midnight hours, and every stepping stone on our road toward forever. You are the one God chose for me, and I am choosing you every single day.

Forever loving you, cheering for you, and holding you close in prayer,
— Derrick 💍
