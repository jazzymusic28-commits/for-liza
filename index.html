To My Dearest Princess Liza,

Having you as my girlfriend brings so much genuine peace to my life. You are my biggest supporter, my prayer partner, and without hesitation the sweetest part of my entire day. I love us, I love everything we are building together, and I love walking this journey with you side by side in spirit every day.

I want to take a moment and step past all the rush of daily life to put down in words how much I truly, deeply love you. When I look at you and think about the person you are, I see an answered prayer that God personally shaped for my life. My feelings for you are not casual, surface-level emotions that change with time or mood—they are rooted in an intentional commitment to honor, cherish, and stand beside you. No matter how many miles, time zones, or oceans stretch between the United States and the Philippines, my love for you does not waver. Distance is merely physical geography; it will never diminish the loyalty, dedication, and affection I carry for you in my heart.

Every single day that passes is one step closer to closing that physical gap. You are not simply someone I talk to to pass the hours—you are the woman I see in my future. You are the one I want to stand at the altar with, take vows before God with, and proudly call my wife. When I look toward tomorrow, my future plans and dreams have you right in the center of them. I am overflowing with joy and gratitude simply knowing that you are my girlfriend today, and I hold that honor with great care and respect.

A Safe Place For Your Heart

I want you to know with complete certainty: you never, ever have to be shy or hesitant with me about anything. Whatever is on your mind—whether it is a tiny fleeting worry, a heavy burden from your shift, family stress, or just a silly random thought—you can bring it to me freely. You don't have to put on a brave face, you don't have to hide when you're tired or sad, and you never have to worry that you are bothering me.

If you have a problem, let's talk about it. If you had a frustrating day, vent to me. If you are anxious about something, let's pray over it together. In this relationship, there is no judgment, no impatience, and no walls. You have my full attention and an open ear whenever you need to let things out.

I admire you so much for the incredible strength, determination, and independence you carry. Watching how hard you push through long, tiring shifts, how disciplined you are, and how you carry your responsibilities with grace leaves me in awe of the woman you are. You have a quiet power and resilience that is nothing short of inspiring. But I want to remind you of something very important: even though you are strong, and even though you know how to stand on your own two feet, you do not have to carry the weight of everything by yourself anymore.

You can lean on me. You are allowed to take off the heavy armor, rest your tired spirit, and let me be your rock and your protector. Let me be the shoulder you rest on, the voice that calms your storms, and the prayer that covers you when your own energy is depleted. Independence is admirable, but partnership is a blessing—and I am here to stand firm with you through every challenge life throws our way.

Seeing You Open Up 😄

It also brings the biggest smile to my face when I think about how much you have opened your heart to me over time! I remember when we first started talking and you were so sweet, polite, and maybe just a tiny bit shy and cautious—keeping all your defenses up like a castle. But seeing you slowly let down those walls, laugh freely with me, share your thoughts, and welcome me into your world has been the most rewarding thing ever. (I knew my charm would eventually win you over, even if you tried to resist at first! 😉) Seeing your authentic self shine through is the greatest gift you could have ever given me.

I thank God for your life again and again. Every single good and perfect gift comes from Him, and the day you came into my life was a divine blessing that I will never take for granted. God knew the exact kind of gentle, pure-hearted, faith-filled companion I needed, and He brought your path across mine. I praise Him for protecting you, for giving you life and health, and for guiding our conversations each day.

I also want to make sure you know this: whenever you want to hop on a video call, or just hear each other on a regular voice call, please never hesitate or feel shy to ask! Just say the word. You never need to wonder if it's okay or if I have time—hearing your soft voice and seeing your beautiful smile completely makes my day. A two-minute voice note or an hour-long call from you instantly brightens up anything going on around me. Your voice brings peace to my spirit, and I always love connecting with you in real time.

My Promise To You 💍

Above everything else, Liza, please know in your bones: I am not here to break your heart, play games, or make you doubt yourself. I am here to protect your heart, support your growth, cherish your soul, and love you with real devotion. You have been hurt or cautious in life, but with me, your heart is in gentle, loyal hands. I am here to walk through every chapter with you—the joyful sunny days, the exhausting midnight hours, and every stepping stone on our road toward forever. You are the one God chose for me, and I am choosing you every single day.

Forever loving you, cheering for you, and holding you close in prayer.
— Derrick ❤️
